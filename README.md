# 🌾 AgriLink — Landing Page Prototype  
### *Wadhwani Entrepreneurship (Module 8.3 — Sales Pitch Activity)*

This repository contains the **official landing page** created for the **“Build Your Sales Pitch”** activity under the **Wadhwani Entrepreneurship Program**.  

The purpose of this landing page is to **promote AgriLink** to potential customers, allowing us to showcase our value proposition, demonstrate our prototype, and simulate how we will attract early users for our startup.

---

## 🌟 About AgriLink

**AgriLink** is a platform designed to reduce **crop wastage** by connecting farmers directly to businesses in need of agricultural produce—such as restaurants, retailers, processors, and local enterprises.  
It enables farmers to sell at fair prices while helping buyers access consistent, reliable crop supply.

---

## 🎯 Purpose of This Landing Page

This website/landing page was created specifically to:

- Display our **Unique Value Proposition** clearly  
- Showcase the **key benefits** of AgriLink  
- Present our **prototype** for customer validation  
- Offer simple **Call-to-Action (CTA)** elements  
- Serve as the digital material for our **sales pitch assignment**  
- Provide an accessible link for mentors and evaluators  

This site is part of the academic requirements for the Wadhwani program and is **not a live commercial product**.
